# Talent Intelligence Hub

## Overview

Talent Intelligence Hub is an AI-powered workforce analytics platform that collects live Data Science and HR job market data from public APIs and transforms it into actionable insights.

The platform is designed to help recruiters, hiring managers, workforce planners, and job seekers understand global hiring trends, skill demand, salary benchmarks, and talent availability.

---

## Objectives

* Collect live Data Science and HR job postings.
* Build a continuously updated job market dataset.
* Analyze hiring trends across countries and cities.
* Track emerging technical and HR skills.
* Provide salary benchmarking.
* Match resumes against current market requirements.
