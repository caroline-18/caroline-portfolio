# Analyze Death Age Difference of Right Handers with Left Handers

## Overview
Handedness has long been studied as a potential factor influencing human lifespan due to neurological, environmental, and behavioral differences. This project performs a **statistical and exploratory data analysis (EDA)** to examine whether a significant difference exists in the age at death between right-handed and left-handed individuals.

The analysis focuses on uncovering patterns using data-driven statistical techniques.

---

## Problem Statement
Previous studies suggest a possible association between handedness and mortality, but findings are often inconsistent or limited by small sample sizes.

**Objective:**  
To statistically analyze and compare the age at death of right-handed and left-handed individuals and determine whether the observed differences are statistically significant.

---

## Methodology
- Cleaned and preprocessed the dataset to handle missing and inconsistent values.
- Conducted exploratory data analysis to visualize age distributions by handedness.
- Applied statistical hypothesis testing to evaluate differences in mortality age.
- Interpreted results using descriptive statistics and visualizations.

---

## Tools & Technologies
- **Programming Language:** Python  
- **Libraries & Frameworks:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, SciPy  
- **Techniques:** Exploratory Data Analysis, Statistical Testing, Data Visualization  

---

## Results
- Identified observable differences in age-at-death distributions between right-handed and left-handed groups.
- Assessed statistical significance using hypothesis testing methods.
- Provided data-driven insights supported by visual and statistical evidence.
