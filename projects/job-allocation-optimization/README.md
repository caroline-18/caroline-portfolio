# Maximizing Profit in Job Allocation Problems: An Optimization Approach

## Overview
Efficient job allocation is a fundamental problem in operations research and resource management, where the goal is to assign tasks to agents in a way that maximizes overall profit under given constraints. This project formulates the job allocation problem as a **mathematical optimization model** and explores algorithmic solutions for optimal decision-making.

---

## Problem Statement
Naive or heuristic-based allocation strategies often lead to suboptimal profit and inefficient resource utilization.

**Objective:**  
To develop an optimization-based framework that maximizes profit while satisfying assignment and operational constraints.

---

## Methodology
- Formulated the job allocation problem using mathematical optimization principles.
- Defined objective functions and constraints representing profit maximization and resource limits.
- Implemented algorithmic solutions in Python to solve the optimization model.
- Analyzed solution optimality and computational efficiency across problem instances.

---

## Tools & Technologies
- **Programming Language:** Python  
- **Libraries & Frameworks:** NumPy, SciPy / Optimization Solvers  
- **Techniques:** Mathematical Modeling, Optimization Algorithms, Operations Research  

---

## Results
- Achieved optimal or near-optimal job assignments under varying constraints.
- Demonstrated improved profit outcomes compared to baseline allocation strategies.
- Validated solution efficiency through computational analysis.
